What is Biceps Muscle ?
The biceps brachii, located on the front of the upper arm, is responsible for elbow flexion (bending the arm) and forearm supination (rotating the palm upward).
Dumbbell Curls (Biceps) Setup: Stand upright, hold a dumbbell in each hand, palms facing forward. Curl: Bend your elbows to lift the dumbbells toward your shoulders. Lower: Slowly lower back to starting position. Repeat: Perform the desired number of reps.
Barbell Curls (Biceps) Setup: Stand upright, hold a barbell with palms facing forward, hands shoulder-width apart. Curl: Bend elbows to lift the bar toward your chest. Lower: Slowly lower back to start. Repeat: Perform the desired number of reps.
Hammer Curls (Biceps / Forearms) Setup: Lie on a decline bench (head lower than hips) and secure your feet. Grip the bar slightly wider than shoulder-width. Lower the Bar: Slowly lower the bar toward your lower chest. Keep elbows at about 45° from your body. Press Up: Push the bar back up until your arms are fully extended. Repeat:Perform the desired number of reps with controlled movements.
