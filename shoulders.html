What is Shoulders Muscle ?
The deltoids are the main muscles of the shoulders, consisting of three heads: anterior (front), lateral (side), and posterior (rear). They are responsible for lifting, rotating, and stabilizing the arms.
Shoulder Press (Deltoids / All Heads) Setup: Sit or stand, hold dumbbells at shoulder height, palms facing forward. Press Up: Push dumbbells overhead until arms are fully extended. Lower: Slowly bring dumbbells back to shoulder height. Repeat: Perform desired reps.
Lateral Raises (Lateral Deltoids / Side Shoulders) Setup: Stand upright, hold dumbbells at sides, palms facing inward. Raise Lift arms to the sides until shoulder height. Lower: Slowly lower back to start. Repeat: Perform desired reps.
Rear Delt Flys (Posterior Deltoids / Rear Shoulders) Setup: Bend forward at hips, dumbbells in hand, palms facing each other. Raise: Lift arms out to the sides until level with shoulders. Lower: Slowly set the plates down. Repeat: Perform desired sets or duration.
