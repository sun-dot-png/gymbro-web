Nutrition is the foundation of fitness. Understanding your nutrients, body mass index, and daily energy expenditure will help you reach your goals faster whether bulking or cutting.

2. BMI (Body Mass Index)

BMI stands for body mass index, a simple tool that uses your height and weight to estimate your body fat.

3. TDEE (Energy Expenditure)

Tdee is Activity + your BMR, your BMR = 2000 that mean if you just layout on the bed do nothing on that day, your body burn 2000 caloriees. But Tdee add activity, for example if your tdee is 2500 that is the number your body can burn with your activities on that day. Another tips is if you want to get more skinny I recommand you to eat less than your tdee around 250 - 500. Anyway if you want to get bigger, I recomand you too eat more than your tdee 250-500.
