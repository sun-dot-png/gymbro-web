Heart Rate Zone ❤️‍🔥

A range of heartbeats per minute (bpm) that measures how hard your heart is working during physical exercise, typically calculated as a percentage of your maximum heart rate (Max HR).

Simple Formula:220 - Your age

Heart rate training is generally split into five progressive levels:

Zone 1 (50% – 60% of Max HR) - Very Light / Warm-Up: Used for warming up, active recovery, and improving basic blood circulation.

Zone 2 (60% – 70% of Max HR) - Light / Endurance & Fat-Burning: Builds aerobic endurance and efficiently utilizes fat as a primary fuel source.

Zone 3 (70% – 80% of Max HR) - Moderate / Aerobic: Improves overall cardiovascular fitness, lung strength, and muscle conditioning.

Zone 4 (80% – 90% of Max HR) - Hard / Threshold & Speed: Increases anaerobic threshold, speed endurance, and carbohydrate metabolism.

Zone 5 (90% – 100% of Max HR) - Maximum / Peak Capacity: Reaches maximum exertion for short, high-intensity intervals meant for well-trained athletes.
