What is Back Muscle ?
The back muscles include the latissimus dorsi (lats), trapezius (traps), rhomboids, and erector spinae. They are responsible for pulling movements, posture, and spinal support.
Pull-Ups (Lats / Upper Back) Setup: Grab a pull-up bar with palms facing away. Hang with arms fully extended. Lower the Bar: Pull your chest toward the bar until your chin passes it. Press Up: Slowly lower to full arm extension. Repeat: Perform the desired number of reps with control.
Bent-Over Rows (Middle Back / Lats) Setup: Hold a barbell or dumbbells shoulder-width apart. Bend forward at hips until torso is nearly parallel to the floor. Lower the Bar: Pull the weight toward your lower chest/upper stomach. Press Up: Slowly lower to starting position. Repeat: Perform the desired number of reps with control.
Lat Pulldowns (Lats / Upper Back) Setup: Sit at a lat pulldown machine and grip the bar wider than shoulder-width. Lift the Bar: Pull the bar down toward your upper chest. Lower the Bar: Slowly let the bar return to starting position. Repeat:Perform the desired number of reps with control.
