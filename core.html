Core / Abs

What is Core / Abs Muscle ?
The core muscles include the rectus abdominis (abs), obliques, and transverse abdominis. They stabilize the spine, support posture, and transfer power between the upper and lower body.
Setup: Lie on your side and place your forearm on the floor with your elbow directly under your shoulder. Extend your legs and stack your feet on top of each other. Hold: Engage your core and glutes, then lift your hips off the floor. Keep your head, torso, and legs in a straight line and hold for the desired duration. Lower: Slowly lower your hips and body back to the floor with control. Repeat:Perform for the desired number of repetitions or duration, then switch sides.
Machine Crunches (Abs / Core) Setup: Sit on the abdominal crunch machine and adjust the seat and pads so they fit comfortably. Place your feet firmly on the footrests and position your upper body against the pads. Crunch: Brace your core and curl your upper body forward, bringing your ribcage toward your pelvis. Focus on contracting your abdominal muscles rather than pulling with your arms. Return: Slowly return to the starting position with control, keeping tension in your abs. Repeat: Perform for the desired number of repetitions and sets.
Leg Raises (Lower Abs / Core) Setup: Lie on your back with legs straight and hands under your glutes for support. Lift: Raise your legs until they are perpendicular to the floor. Lower: Slowly lower your legs without letting them touch the ground. Repeat:Perform the desired number of reps.
