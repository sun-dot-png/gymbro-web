What is Legs Muscle ?
The leg muscles include the quadriceps (front thigh), hamstrings (back thigh), glutes (buttocks), calves, and adductors/abductors. They are responsible for movement, stability, and support of the lower body.
Squats (Quadriceps / Glutes / Hamstrings) Setup: Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, back straight, chest up. Lower: Bend knees and hips to lower your body as if sitting on a chair. Press Up: Push through your heels to return to standing. Repeat:Perform the desired number of reps.
>
Lunges (Quadriceps / Glutes / Hamstrings) Setup: Stand upright, feet together. Step forward with one leg. Lower: Bend both knees until the back knee almost touches the ground. Press Up: Push through the front heel to return to start. Repeat: Alternate legs for desired reps.
Calf Raises (Calves) Setup: Stand upright with feet shoulder-width apart, can hold a wall or dumbbells for balance. Lift: Raise your heels off the ground, standing on your toes. Lower: Slowly lower heels back to the floor. Repeat:Perform the desired number of reps.
