What is Forearms Muscle ?
The forearms consist of multiple muscles that control the wrist, hand, and fingers. They are responsible for grip strength, wrist movements, and stabilizing the hand during lifting.
Wrist Curls (Forearms / Flexors) Setup: Sit on a bench, hold a dumbbell in each hand with palms up. Curl: Flex your wrists to lift the dumbbells. Lower: Slowly lower back to start. Repeat: Perform desired reps.
Reverse Wrist Curls (Forearms / Extensors) Setup: Sit on a bench, hold a dumbbell in each hand with palms facing down. Curl: Extend your wrists to lift the dumbbells. Lower: Slowly lower back to start. Repeat: Perform desired reps.
Plate Pinches (Forearms / Grip Strength) Setup: Hold two weight plates together (smooth sides out) between your fingers and thumb. Hold: Keep the plates pinched and lift them off the ground. Lower: Slowly set the plates down. Repeat: Perform desired sets or duration.
