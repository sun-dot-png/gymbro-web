What is Triceps Muscle ?
The triceps brachii, located on the back of the upper arm, is responsible for extending the elbow (straightening the arm).
Triceps Dips (Triceps / Back Arm) Setup: Place hands on a bench or parallel bars, legs extended forward. Lower: Bend elbows to lower your body toward the ground. Press Up: Push through your palms to straighten arms and lift body back up. Repeat: Perform desired reps.
Triceps Pushdowns (Triceps / Back Arm) Setup: Stand at a cable machine, hold the bar/rope with palms facing down. Press Down: Extend elbows to push the bar/rope toward thighs. Return: Slowly allow the bar/rope to rise back to start. Repeat: Perform desired reps.
Overhead Triceps Extension (Triceps / Back Arm) Setup: Hold a dumbbell or barbell overhead with both hands, arms extended. Lower: Bend elbows to lower the weight behind your head. Press Up: Extend elbows to lift the weight back overhead. Repeat: Perform desired reps.
